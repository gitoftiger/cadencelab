CadenceLab — Support

CadenceLab is a running app for iPhone and Apple Watch, made for half marathons, marathons and trail races.

Contact: rexletiger@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 business days.

Getting started

  1. Open CadenceLab on your iPhone and allow access to Apple Health when asked. The app reads your runs and related health data to build your analytics.
  2. Install the CadenceLab app on your Apple Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone (My Watch → Available Apps).
  3. Set up your race or training run, then start it from your Apple Watch.

Features

  • Fueling & hydration reminders on your wrist during long runs and races.
  • Cadence & form feedback while you run.
  • Grade-based race pacing that adjusts your target pace for climbs and descents.
  • Analytics & recovery built from your Apple Health data.

Common questions

  • The app shows no runs or data. On your iPhone open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → CadenceLab and turn on the data types. Then reopen the app.
  • The Watch app doesn't appear. Make sure your Apple Watch is paired and updated, then install CadenceLab from the Watch app on your iPhone.
  • Reminders don't vibrate on my watch. Check that the watch isn't in Silent mode or a Focus that blocks notifications, and that haptics are on (Watch Settings → Sounds & Haptics).
  • Restore my purchase. In the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.
  • Cancel a subscription. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → CadenceLab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date (or before a free trial ends) to avoid being charged.

CadenceLab Pro

CadenceLab is free to download. CadenceLab Pro is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription (with a 7-day free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple ID.

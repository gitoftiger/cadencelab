CadenceLab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

CadenceLab ("the app") helps you train for and run races on iPhone and Apple Watch. We take your privacy seriously: the app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your data to any server operated by us.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, CadenceLab reads data from Apple Health, such as workouts, distance, heart rate, cadence and related activity data, to show your training analytics, pacing and recovery. Health data read or written by the app:

  • is processed only on your iPhone and Apple Watch;
  • is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or marketing;
  • is never stored in iCloud or on any server by the app.

When you record a run with the CadenceLab Apple Watch app, the workout (for example duration, distance and heart rate) is saved to Apple Health, with your permission, so it appears alongside your other workouts.

You can change or remove access at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → CadenceLab.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps exchange your run settings and run data directly with each other. Nothing is sent to the internet.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them only for reminders you set up, such as fueling and hydration reminders during a run.

Purchases

CadenceLab Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Settings

Your preferences and race plans are stored only on your devices.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
